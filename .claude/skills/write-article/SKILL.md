# Title

- The title, Description and Icon of the article are what the user sees first. 
- Must be straight to the point. Must briefly communicate what value this article adds to the reader.
- Title is the main selling point, followed by description for further content. Icon or else an illustration is used to provide quick 
easu cognitive context to the user.
- Title and Description should communicate with as less context as possible while beign eye catchy and giving correct context.

# Summary

- Summary in a few lines, 1 or 2 small paragraphs summarize what this article covers and challenges.

# Intented audience

- This is to provide more context to the reader about who this article is for.

# Content

- Explain what this article tries to fix. Always give a real world example.
- Explain why this bad practice is wrong in a few bullet points.
- Give the solution, and what is solved with it.
- Compare a Before & After real world case.
- Add a sum up at the end.
